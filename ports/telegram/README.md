# Terminal Noir for Telegram

[Telegram Desktop](https://desktop.telegram.org) themes with amber unread badges, buttons and links. `terminal-noir.tdesktop-theme` (dark) brings the noir city wallpaper and `terminal-noir-paper.tdesktop-theme` (light) the paper one; the `.tdesktop-palette` files hold the same colors without a wallpaper. The key mapping is adapted from [catppuccin/telegram](https://github.com/catppuccin/telegram) (MIT).

![Terminal Noir for Telegram](../../assets/screenshots/telegram.png)

## Install

In Telegram Desktop, open **Settings > Chat Settings**, click the three-dot menu and choose **Load from file**, then pick one of the files:

```sh
terminal-noir.tdesktop-theme          # colors and the noir wallpaper
terminal-noir-paper.tdesktop-theme    # colors and the paper wallpaper
terminal-noir.tdesktop-palette        # colors only, keeps your wallpaper
```

Telegram applies it right away; **Keep changes** saves it.
